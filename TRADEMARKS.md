marks and artwork** are not covered by that licence. This page says what you may
and may not reuse, and credits the artwork properly.

## The Go Gopher

Any Gopher artwork in this repository — including derivative poses such as the
shepherd — is used under CC-BY 3.0 **with attribution to Renée French**, as that
licence requires. Derivative works are permitted by CC-BY 3.0; the attribution is
not optional and must travel with any copy.

The Go Gopher is not a Google trademark, but **"Go" and the Go logo are**
trademarks of Google LLC. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by

## The Runink marks

"Runink", the Runink logo (the dog-head mark) and the Australian Shepherd mascot
are held by **Runink**. No licence in this repository grants any right to use
them. They are present so that a reader can tell this is the project's own
release — the same reason the Runink River project states for its marks.

If you fork `runi`, you may use the code freely under BSD-3-Clause, but please
replace the artwork and do not use the Runink name in a way that suggests the
fork is ours.
